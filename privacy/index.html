Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

FLIKS TV Publisher ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the FLIKS TV channel to publish videos to their own accounts.

What the app accesses

  • Basic TikTok profile information (user.info.basic) to identify the connected account.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos (video.upload, video.publish) to that account.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app has no server, database, analytics, or advertising, and it collects no information about other users or viewers.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. Videos and captions are sent only to TikTok and YouTube when publishing.

Removal

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings under Security & permissions, Apps and services. Deleting the local token file removes all stored data.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/arsenicx778/tiktok-callback.
